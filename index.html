ASCII CITY // CONTROLS & SYSTEMS 

    

      

        MOVEMENT
        
WASD / ↑↓ — walk   SHIFT — run
MOUSE — look (click to lock, or drag)
← → — turn   PGUP/PGDN — look up/down

        INTERACT [E]
        
Answer the phone · enter any building · call the lift · leave by the door · use a telephone relay · hail a cab at a rank · board a sky taxi

        TRAVEL
        
SHIFT+Y — locate nearest ground taxi rank (t)
Y — locate nearest sky taxi (T)
Y (in a cab) — ask the driver to pull over
Y (in a sky taxi) — emergency set-down
METRO — walk into a station (M), climb the stairs, cross the bridge and wait on the island platform. Walk on when the doors open, walk off at another stop.
RELAYS — discover booths (R); the directory lets you transmit to any relay you've found.

      

      

        VIEW
        
M — map zoom: LOCAL → DISTRICT → SECTOR → CITY
G — toggle coloured glyph backing (classic sparse ASCII)
V — change character resolution
C — ASCII CITY // LIVE camera feeds (1–6, ← →)

        MODES
        
T — auto tour (A* to the nearest landmark)
P — empty city (no people, no traffic)

        MUSIC
        
SHIFT+N — music on/off   [ ] — volume

        MENUS
        
1–9, 0 — choose   ESC / BACKSPACE — back / close
SPACE — skip the opening

        THE CITY
        
8.2 km across · 16 sectors · 256 districts · 65,536 blocks · named streets · 8 landmark buildings + the Signal Keeper · 3 monorail lines
